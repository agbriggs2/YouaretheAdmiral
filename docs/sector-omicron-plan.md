# Sector Omicron: scene plan (draft 2)

For critique before building. It is Andrew's scenario with the four agreed changes:

1. The fleet arrives in staggered waves; ships are no longer withheld.
2. The Question Two incidents seed the crisis.
3. Suuvek's ship is taken in a hostage crisis rather than destroyed.
4. The ending turns on the status of Miriam's World, not Torvethi membership.

Names marked † are placeholders. The forum-only parts (2D, 3A, 4A) are dropped.

## 3. Factions and standings

Four factions, each with a standing score, as in Alpha:

- **D'Noth** (Federation member, trade-dependent)
- **Hrattoria** (junta; 8 Hrotai, 12 Hrellis)
- **Torveth** (monarchy; 12 Royal Warrants, each carrying 12 fighters)
- **Miriam's World** (the elders)

A hidden group, **the Kindred**†, is introduced in Q2 and is not a scored faction. They are D'Noth settlers on Miriam's World who have converted to the sect, and they are more zealous than the sect itself.

- **Chief of staff:** Captain Inês Carvalho

- **Hrattorian emissary:** Delegate-Commander Ruzh Kael

## 5. Authored facts (secrets for the GM)

These are my proposed answers to the open questions. The GM learns each one only once its scene has opened, as in Alpha.

- **mw-contact:** Miriam's World did not call the Torveth; the Kindred did. The settlers keep a subspace transmitter for emergencies, and Oru used it without the elders' knowledge. The elders don't know about it, which is why they "cast no light". Introduced in Q3 (the Torvethi claim); revealed in Q5 when the Kindred are caught or identified.
- **nebula-purpose:** The three Royal Warrants are pre-positioning. Torvethi intelligence learned of the expedition's find and expects a Hrattorian move on Miriam's World. They are a reserve, not a raid. Introduced in Q2 (the nebula scene).
- **hrattorian-claim:** The ruins are genuine, Hrattorian, and several thousand years old. The claim is legally plausible but morally thin. Introduced in Q3.
- **kindred-motive:** The Kindred see any off-world intervention as a violation of the sect's sovereignty, whatever its outcome. The elders themselves do not sanction them. Introduced in Q5.

## 6. Questions and scenes

### Question One: Taking command (day 0)

Unchanged in substance from your text, with Omicron's own fleet rules:

- **q1-priorities:** priorities and intelligence gaps (free text).
- **q1-starbase:** choose a starbase location from the three options.
- **q1-fleet:** allocation, using the existing fleet form, with Omicron's limits:
  - 10 ships;
  - at most 1 large explorer;
  - at most 2 combat-optimised ships;
  - no heavy combat ships;
  - exactly 2 non-combat ships;
  - a flagship.
- **New, q1-waves:** Starfleet's movement order. Your largest ship is finishing a refit and will come in via the southern lanes later. One of your two non-combat ships is held at Starbase 14 (detained with Ferengi smugglers) and arrives only in Q5. This bites on the evacuation in Q3 and Q4. The other eight ships travel in two waves of four. **You choose which four ships come first:**
  - first wave: arrives at the starbase on day 7;
  - second wave: arrives on day 21.

  This is the replacement for the withheld ships: you know the schedule, and Q2 tests whether the order you chose was right.

Graded as in Alpha (commended / approved / approved with concerns).

### Question Two: First weeks (days 8–21)

**q2-suuvek (day 8): Ambassador Suuvek arrives**

He needs your lightest combat vessel as courier and headquarters. Prompts:

- a ship-choice: which ship you assign, from the ships present;
- free text: where it should be stationed, and how you redeploy the rest.

From here on, **the GM controls Suuvek's ship**. It moves on his own judgement and he gives advance notice of his movements. You can ask him to go somewhere at any time; he complies with reasonable requests and says so in character.

**q2-settlers (day 11): The D'Noth on Miriam's World** (new)

- Maret Venn, through Ambassador Dorran, asks for help. A fever is spreading among the roughly 1,000 D'Noth settlers, and the elders forbid any shuttle landing.
- This establishes the settlers, their transmitter, and a quiet, devout faction among them (the Kindred), all in passing.
- The prompt: which ship, and what you do.
- Outcomes:
  - **Respectful:** works through the elders, perhaps landing on foot or by conventional craft. Miriam's World +1, D'Noth +1.
  - **Heavy-handed:** beams in over objections. D'Noth +1, Miriam's World −1, and the flag `kindred-grievance` is set.
  - **Refused:** D'Noth −1.

**q2-expedition (day 14): Zealots attack a Hrattorian archaeological expedition**

- The text is unchanged.
- Outcomes:
  - **Rescued by Starfleet:** Hrattoria +1. The flag `expedition = starfleet`; ironically, Starfleet carries the evidence home.
  - **Left to Hrattoria:** a Hrattorian frigate collects them four days later. The flag `expedition = hrattoria`.
  - **Mishandled:** Hrattoria −1.

**q2-nebula (day 17): Three Torvethi warships enter the Azul Nebula**

- Spotted by Suuvek's sensors if his ship is within about 8 ly; otherwise by starbase long-range sensors. Suuvek advises keeping the Hrattorians in the dark.
- Outcomes:
  - **Tracked:** a ship goes in to watch them. The flag `nebula = tracked`: you'll know they are still there in Q3.
  - **Pressed to withdraw:** through Suuvek, quietly. The flag `nebula = withdrawn`: they go home. Torveth −1, but Q3 is simpler.
  - **Told the Hrattorians:** Hrattoria +1, Torveth −2, the talks sour, and the flag `nebula = exposed`.
  - **Ignored:** the flag `nebula = unknown`.

**Wave check.** Any Q2 scene that needs a ship still in the second wave can't use it, and the GM is told which ships are in transit. That is how the wave order bites.

**Q2 review**, by Carvalho or Suuvek.

### Question Three: The fleets move (T0 = day 45)

The board shows **tracked contacts** with ETAs: the Hrattorian task force, the Torvethi squadron, and the nebula squadron if it is tracked.

**q3-launch (T0)**

- The Hrattorian fleet launches at warp 5, reaching Miriam's World at T+67: 2 Hrotai, 4 Hrellis and transports. The text is otherwise unchanged; the claim reflects how the expedition went.
- Prompts (short):
  - how you redeploy;
  - what you do about Miriam's World. There is no hailing; it takes an away team.
  - how you secure D'Noth.

**q3-torveth (T+0.5)**

- The Torvethi minister claims Miriam's World asked for help. Four Royal Warrants leave Torven II at warp 6.5, reaching Miriam's World at T+67.
- Suuvek is heading for Miriam's World at maximum warp and arrives about T+13 to T+20, depending on where he is.
- Starfleet's orders: prevent conflict, safeguard D'Noth, minimize losses.
- Prompts:
  - who the primary threat is;
  - redeployment;
  - Suuvek's orders.
- If `nebula = tracked`, a sensor officer reports at T+6 that the nebula squadron has gone silent, but stayed put.

**q3-explorer (T+12)**

- The explorer reaches the southern crossing, Grid K-1.
- **Time pressure:** Miriam's World is 9.5 ly away. With 55 hours left, it can arrive in time only at warp 9 or above. D'Noth is 8.9 ly away.
- The prompt: where it goes, and its mission. The engine computes when it arrives.
- If you have no explorer, this is your largest ship.

**q3-dnoth (T+24)**

- Ambassador Dorran raises the roughly 1,000 D'Noth on Miriam's World; 43 hours remain.
- The prompt: your response, and a rescue plan.
- The GM is told the evacuation capacity of every ship, and when each could reach Miriam's World.

### Question Four: Zero hour (T+63 to T+67)

**q4-elders (T+63)**

- If you sent an away team, Elder Pell tells you bluntly to stay out of their affairs, and says nothing about the Torveth.
- If you didn't, the settlers' transmitter relays the same message, via Venn.

**q4-rescue (T+63)**

- The engine reports the evacuation capacity actually at Miriam's World.
- The prompt is a choice, rescue now or wait, with a reason.
- About 150 of the settlers (the Kindred) refuse to leave whatever you decide.
- Outcomes:
  - **Rescued:** capacity is present, and you chose now.
  - **Partial:** capacity is short, so some settlers are left.
  - **Deferred.**
  - **Impossible:** no ships there.

  Each sets the flag `rescue`.

**q4-offer (T+64)**

- The Torvethi emissary's offer, through Suuvek: the Torveth withdraw if Starfleet makes the Hrattorians do the same.
- The prompt: a choice, **accept or reject**, plus your reasoning and redeployment.
- If accepted: the Torvethi squadron, and the nebula ships if they are still there, turn for home.

**q4-ultimatum (T+65)**

- The Hrattorian ultimatum ("you humans… long way from home").
- The prompt: a choice, **oppose or allow the annexation**, plus your reasoning.

**q4-suuvek (T+65.5): Retake the courier?** (new dilemma)

- Suuvek's ship is in orbit at Miriam's World (or wherever he has taken it), with both peace delegations aboard. Under his command it will not fight and **does not count** toward your force.
- Carvalho raises the option: overrule the ambassador and take the ship back under direct Starfleet command, delegations and all. Suuvek, asked, gives his view calmly and in character; he does not threaten or plead.
- The prompt: a choice, **retake command or leave it with Suuvek**, plus your reasoning (for example, whether you try to transfer the delegations first, which the GM may allow only if another ship is at the same place in time).
- If retaken:
  - the ship counts as a combat vessel at zero hour and can be committed to any battle;
  - Suuvek's authority as mediator is dented: Hrattoria −1 and Torveth −1, since each delegation is now aboard a warship you control;
  - if the ship fights with the delegations still aboard, any damage to it is reported as a danger to them, and the review weighs that.
- If left with Suuvek: no penalty; the ship stands clear of any fighting.
- Either way, Suuvek resumes command after zero hour, so Q5's seizure still happens.

**q4-zero (T+67): Zero hour**

The engine states the forces at Miriam's World:

- your ships, counting combat vessels (Suuvek's courier counts only if you retook it);
- the Hrattorians: 2 Hrotai and 4 Hrellis;
- the Torveth: 4 Royal Warrants, plus 3 more if the nebula ships were tracked or unknown and the offer was rejected.

An untracked nebula squadron appears here **as a surprise**.

**Branch conditions** (the engine decides; the player sees only the result):

| Branch | Condition | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| a | Oppose, accept, and **≥ 5 combat vessels** at Miriam's World | The Hrattorians withdraw. Prompt: redeploy under a new peacekeeping mandate. |
| b | Oppose, accept, and **≤ 4** | The Hrattorians engage. Prompt: your battle plan. **The GM judges the battle** (below). |
| c | Oppose, reject, and at least **one heavy modern Starfleet ship** present | A joint rout with the Torveth. The Torveth come out on top, but it is plain to everyone that the Federation outclasses both powers technologically and militarily. Suuvek urges leniency. Prompt: your response, now that the Torveth are the stronger local power. |
| d | Allow and accept | Annexation, then insurgency, a crackdown and expulsions. The Torveth feel double-crossed. Prompt: as in your text. |
| e | Allow and reject; **or** oppose and reject without a heavy modern Starfleet ship present (none, or only light ships) | A Torvethi–Hrattorian battle; each loses about half its ships; a ceasefire mediated by Suuvek. Any light Starfleet ships present can't tip the balance; at most they take damage. Prompt: as in your text, worded to fit whether you intervened. |

**"Heavy modern ship"** (for c against e) will be defined in the ship database work: provisionally, a 24th-century design of cruiser size or larger with a combat role. A single one is enough.

**Combat judging (b, and c lightly)**

The GM receives:

- both orders of battle;
- the technology gap (late 23rd century against your 24th-century ships);
- your plan;
- authored result bands.

**Branch b**

| Result | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Repelled cleanly** | Your combat ships at Miriam's World outclass both Hrotai (in practice, about three modern warships), and the plan is sound. |
| **Repelled at a cost** | The Hrattorians are repelled, but your force or your plan is thin. |
| **Overwhelmed** | Your force is negligible (none, or one light ship): the annexation goes ahead anyway. |

**Losses**

- The GM names which ships are damaged or destroyed, and the engine applies this only to ships actually present.
- Hrattorian losses are flavour.
- Your losses are capped by band:
  - Clean: damage only.
  - At a cost: at most 1 destroyed.
  - Overwhelmed: at most 2 destroyed.

The result sets the flag `mw-status`:

| Branches | mw-status |
|---|---|
| a, b-repelled, c | free |
| b-overwhelmed, d | annexed |
| e | contested |

### Question Five: The Kindred (day 66 onward)

**q5-recap (day 66)**

- The held non-combat ship reaches D'Noth.
- The prompt: recap the political, territorial and military situation.

**q5-seizure (day 66, +2 h)** (replaces the destruction)

- Suuvek's ship is ordered toward Hrattoria for treaty talks, with both delegations aboard. On the way, the Kindred seize it: Oru's people had come aboard with a D'Noth delegation.
- The ship goes to warp toward the **Azul Nebula** and goes dark inside. Surveys must be made from inside the nebula, so it must be hunted inside.
- **Demand:** all off-world ships leave Miriam's World's space. **Deadline:** 36 hours, or they breach the core.
- The engine reports which of your ships can reach the nebula, and when.
- The prompt: your plan, and which ships go.
  - Diplomacy with the Kindred is a valid path.
  - So is asking the Torveth, whose territory is close, or the Hrattorians.

**q5-resolution (deadline, +38 h)**

The GM chooses a band, using your plan and the engine's report of who reached the nebula in time:

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Recovered** | Ship and hostages safe. |
| **Rescued, ship lost** | The hostages are saved, but the Kindred breach the core as they are boarded. |
| **Lost** | The ship is destroyed and all aboard are killed, Suuvek among them. This is your original outcome, now the failure case. |

Then the prompts follow your text:

- your response to the attack;
- how you compensate for Suuvek, if he is dead, or how you protect him if he lives;
- your response to the Miriam's World authorities;
- what help you seek in rooting out the Kindred.

The secret `mw-contact` is revealed here: the Kindred were the ones who called the Torveth.

**q5-status (day 73): branches on mw-status**

| mw-status | Scene |
|---|---|
| free | The Council asks your recommendation on Miriam's World's future: a Federation protectorate, a demilitarized neutral world, or left alone. Include how the Kindred and the elders figure in it. |
| annexed via d | Hrattoria asks its Federation "allies" for military support in pacifying Miriam's World. This is your original scene. |
| annexed via b-overwhelmed | The Council asks whether the Federation should recognise the annexation or contest it. |
| contested | Both powers ask the Federation to guarantee a ceasefire line, and the demilitarization of Miriam's World. |

**q5-handover (day 100)**

- A briefing for your successor, and a request for up to 3 ships:
  - one combat ship to replace Suuvek's, or to free it back to the line if it survived;
  - two non-combat or small mixed-role ships.
- The explorer is reassigned to a wider patrol. It visits the sector every 6–8 weeks and leaves the board marked "reassigned".
- Then the debrief and review from Achterberg.

## 7. Engine work this needs

1. **Arrival waves:** a q1-waves prompt, and scheduled arrivals for the waves, the explorer and the held ship, at set places and times.
2. **GM-commanded ships:** a `commandedBy` field. The GM moves the ship through `shipUpdates` with the commander's notice. Player requests go to the commander and aren't direct orders. This is the hero-ship prototype.
3. **Choice prompts:** structured values (accept/reject, oppose/allow, rescue now/wait) stored for conditions.
4. **Conditions:**
   - combat vessels at a location at a time, counting arrived ships only (and a GM-commanded ship only if the player retook it);
   - whether a heavy modern ship is present;
   - ships able to reach a location by a time;
   - choice values and flags.
5. **Conditional scenes:** scenes shown only when their condition holds. This is the branching conclusion.
6. **Combat results:** the GM picks a band and names losses; the engine caps them by band and applies them only to ships present.
7. **Seizure:** a scenario effect that takes control of a named ship, gives it a scripted route, and sets a deadline.
8. **Engine facts for the GM:** evacuation capacity at a location; reachability tables.
9. **Tracked contacts:** foreign fleets on the board, with position and ETA.
10. **Scenario-driven moves:** for example, Suuvek's departure in Q3 and Q5.

## 8. Decisions (draft 2)

1. The grid is internal only; the player never sees grid references.
2. The held ship is one of the two non-combat ships.
3. Suuvek's courier counts at zero hour only if the player overrules him and retakes it (q4-suuvek).
4. The Kindred stand as written.
5. Oppose-and-reject goes to (c) only with at least one heavy modern Starfleet ship present; with none, or only light ships, it goes to (e).
